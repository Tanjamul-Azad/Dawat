## Prompt 1 — garden canvas

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original mobile digital wedding invitation background, portrait 2:3 composition. Create a refined hand-painted Bengali wedding garden on warm ivory cotton paper. This is ARTWORK ONLY, not a photograph of a card, no device, no UI, no border of a physical sheet, no letters, no typography, no logos. The design itself fills the entire canvas. Thin wine-red Jamdani woven ornament at the far edges; graceful jasmine and magnolia vines entering from the upper corners; a softly painted pale courtyard arch and lotus pond with tiny brass lanterns concentrated in the LOWER QUARTER, a few red petals. Restrained Bengal textile/watercolor aesthetic, hand-drawn archival invitation illustration, muted sage, aged brass, oxblood and creamy ivory. Keep the central upper 65 percent mostly luminous empty ivory for live names and dates; fine vines only at far edges. Beautiful, sophisticated, intimate, airy, detailed without clutter, flat pigment and delicate lines, no photorealistic 3D, no shiny gold, no generic floral stock art, no people. Bottom painted scenery should fade naturally into the ivory space above. This original art will be used as one continuous immersive digital invitation scene, not a card thumbnail.

## Prompt 2 — transparent companion vine

Use case: illustration-story. Generate a NEW companion botanical illustration, using the previous garden image ONLY as style/palette reference, not as an edit target. Asset for the edges of an immersive Bengali digital wedding invitation. Transparent background, actual alpha. A single gracefully curving long jasmine vine intertwined with small cream magnolia flowers, muted sage leaves, a few wine-red buds, delicate fine antique-brass stems. Portrait 2:3, asymmetrical vine travels from upper left toward lower middle, open and airy with fine separated stems, edges clean, all foliage within canvas, restrained watercolor and archival hand-drawn linework on NO PAPER BACKGROUND. Same ivory, sage and oxblood pigment as the garden reference. No text, no border rectangle, no people, no device, no UI, no shadows cast onto an opaque backdrop. Lots of fully transparent negative space. Sophisticated wedding stationery illustration, not a logo and not clipart.

## Integration

The garden paints the opening/names scene and the closing of the complete digital card, not an image of a physical card inside a webpage. The transparent vine coordinates the woven folio and interior chapters. Side stitching, live typography, reveal motion, date ribbon and functional response leaf are implemented in code. Photography remains the previously generated fictional sample until replaced by the customer's own photos. Use the photo-free edition when photographs are not supplied.

The result is an implemented candidate for review, not final visual approval or a measured production release. See `../build-evidence/digital-card-v5.md`.

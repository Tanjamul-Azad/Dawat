# ImageGen — original first-template artwork prompts

Finalized 19 October 2026. These briefs produce raster artwork/concept stills, not functioning UI or video. Use with the [AI Studio build prompt](google-ai-studio-build.md) and [Figma/Claude design prompt](figma-ai-claude-design.md).

The Digital Yes is the main experience-quality reference; Dribbble informs composition and hierarchy; Pinterest informs stationery/material ideas. The actual artwork must be original. Keep live names, dates, venues and buttons out of generated images. Check selected output, mobile crop, provenance/usage terms and optimization before production use.

Latest priority: **the full invitation template is the main selling point**, not just the cover or dashboard. Product languages are only Bangla and English. Generate no baked-in customer names/translated words: semantic live text will handle both scripts with safe layout. Make a coherent original art family for opening, inside dividers, photo framing and closing; a beautiful isolated cover does not finish the template. Provide deliberately composed no-photo variants and safe quiet text areas for long Bangla names. Source inspiration supplies principles, never copied curtain scenes or competitor photos/music.

For motion exploration, produce original storyboard stills of a woven-paper jacket, seal release, layered paper opening and open inside composition. These are keyframes/asset studies, not a working animation or video. Keep separate layers where useful, limited art density and phone-safe framing. All important text/actions stay outside artwork and remain available under reduced motion/failed media. Final artwork selection remains subject to comparison; do not assume the present generated portrait art has passed commercial qualification.

## Prompt 1 — first template portrait artwork

```text
Use case: illustration
Asset type: premium portrait wedding-invitation artwork for a mobile-first Bangladesh invitation website
Primary request: an original “Jamdani Ivory” direction: exquisitely crafted ivory woven-paper stationery with a desi/Bengali heart, restrained botanical illustration, a deep wine envelope/seal and fine antique-brass accents.
Style/medium: sophisticated handmade stationery with tactile textile/paper detail and intentional editorial composition; not clip art or a screenshot of a website.
Composition/framing: tall portrait image for a phone; generous quiet central space for live couple names and lower space for live date/location. Decoration mainly at edges. Preserve safe margins for 320–390px layouts and a photo-free version. Think of coherent independently movable layers rather than a busy flat border.
Lighting/mood: soft natural studio light, intimate and celebratory, gentle paper shadows and no glare under text.
Materials/textures: subtle original weave, fine handmade grain, delicate controlled botanical detail; no copied motif/whole scene from a named pin or competitor.
Text: none. No letters, names, dates, logos, UI controls, pseudo-script or watermark.
Constraints: original composition, no imposed religious symbols, no fake customer photos. Real Bangla/English content will be semantic text layered above the art.
Avoid: neon gradients, random AI flourishes, excessive glitter, noisy texture, crowded ornaments, generic square-panel layout and competitor imitation.
```

## Prompt 2 — transparent decorative layer

```text
Use case: illustration
Asset type: transparent botanical/textile ornament for the same original invitation
Primary request: one refined asymmetrical edge/corner branch with an original woven-floral accent, coordinating with Jamdani Ivory's ivory/wine/olive/brass palette.
Composition/framing: upright ornament with clean natural outer bounds; enough separation from the central name/text area; suitable for subtle mobile paper-layer motion.
Style/medium: delicate illustrated stationery ornament with controlled handmade detail.
Background: genuinely transparent; preserve alpha, do not draw a checkerboard backdrop.
Text: none.
Constraints: no copied artwork, logos, religious icon, clipped outer leaves or UI. Animation and positioning will be built separately.
```

## Prompt 3 — optional hero still / video storyboard reference

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: original stationery hero still; optional keyframe reference for a separately produced video
Primary request: an original premium Bengali wedding invitation/envelope scene related to Jamdani Ivory, with tactile paper depth, restrained botanical/weave details and a deep wine envelope/seal.
Composition/framing: mobile portrait composition first; retain the main subject and quiet live-text areas. Afterwards make a deliberate desktop landscape variant with negative space for website headline/CTA, rather than cropping away the portrait subject.
Lighting/mood: soft cinematic daylight, quietly joyful, refined natural shadows.
Text: none; no fake names, buttons, logos or dashboard screenshots.
Constraints: original art, no competitor scene or customer photographs. Recognizable hierarchy at phone size. The website renders its own names, copy and actions.
Avoid: generic floating-device mockups, excessive props, busy floral walls, illegible tiny lettering and anything requiring a mouse to understand.
```

This produces a **still**. Actual video requires a supported animation/video workflow and original/licensed sources. Use a poster/still if video is slow or unhelpful. Motion must work with ordinary touch scrolling, with reduced-motion/static fallback and no autoplay audio.

Save selected project assets inside the workspace, preserve originals/versioned variants and create appropriately sized optimized derivatives. Record prompt/source, dimensions/bytes/alpha, intended role and usage terms. Do not flatten guest names, facts, RSVP fields or buttons into the artwork.

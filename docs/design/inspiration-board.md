## Board structure for P03

Curate roughly 12–18 purposeful references across four groups: storefront/editorial UI, maker/client workspace, desi stationery/art, and motion/opening. Prefer a small explained selection over hundreds of unrelated pins.

For each selected reference record: source URL/title/creator, the exact visual principle worth studying, the surface it informs, what our original design will change, and rights/provenance status. Links are sufficient for this planning shortlist; do not copy unlicensed image files into the production asset directory. Keep any permitted study captures clearly separate from our original art.

Suggested search topics: premium wedding website, invitation builder UI, wedding guest dashboard, Bengali wedding stationery, Jamdani motifs, original alpana composition, marigold invitation, botanical envelope liner, wax-seal paper invitation and architectural arch invitation. No single religious/cultural motif is a default for all customers.

## Proposed synthesis, subject to actual visual exploration

One coherent Bengali wedding atelier: editorial scale, original desi line/illustration work, paper depth, carefully chosen display typography, excellent Bangla body text and expressive but controlled motion. Theme art can be dramatic; forms and control hierarchy remain reliable. Avoid importing a generic dashboard skin or combining disconnected fashionable effects.

Deliver a developed direction with palette/type/spacing, one original invitation cover/body, one storefront composition and one client workspace composition. Include mobile views and rationale. Then refine the high-fidelity system using the [design roadmap](design-roadmap.md); this source shortlist does not satisfy the visual-design gate by itself.
